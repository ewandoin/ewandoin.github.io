A localized take on technical outdoor footwear, designed around a Vibram Baltra outsole using strictly locally available materials. Featuring hand-treated water-resistant leather and Cordura 500D uppers, this pair was pattern-drafted, cut, stitched, and lasted entirely by hand to prove functional sportstyle production within local sourcing constraints. Vibram soles are mostly sold here for repairs, so I also wanted the finished shoe to look as good as possible, with the aim of working with Vibram properly in future.
