ERIK YVON: Footwear Concepts

Footwear design and brand extension, with Erik Yvon

A collaborative design project exploring what footwear would look like under the ERIK YVON label. Driven by the brand’s apparel style guide, these concepts translate bold colour, queer culture and Mauritian heritage into expressive, technical 3D footwear models.
