ERIK YVON

Footwear Concept Design

ERIK YVON

Footwear Concept Design

  • Sketching
  • Illustrator
  • Photoshop
  • Blender

Developed under the creative guidance of Erik Yvon, this project explores a hypothetical footwear extension for the fashion label. I produced the end-to-end design process, translating trend forecasting and brand DNA into technical 3D reality. The high-fashion stiletto concept embeds motifs central to the label’s storytelling, bold color, queer culture, and Mauritian heritage. Its hero feature is a modular beaded heel, which can be swapped around to various colours. There’s also an accompanying graphic I produced for promotional use.

The second concept interprets the brand’s identity through a sporty-yet-chic sneaker lens, playing off a classic, versatile Prada silhouette. Designed to transition seamlessly into casual everyday wear, I sketched, designed, and 3D-modeled the silhouette, using Erik’s signature flower motifs as interchangeable, customizable elements. The layered upper features a multi-textured spacer mesh, mocked up in the vibrant, unapologetic color palettes that define the label’s signature apparel.
