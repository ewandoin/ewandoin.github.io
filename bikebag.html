Custom Bike Bag

Design & Prototyping Modular Bike Bag

Materials:
Water Repellent Waxed Canvas
Lightweight Reinforced Mesh for Airflow
Nylon Zipper - Glossy Chromium Neodymium Magnets

  • Sketching
  • Pattern Making
  • Cut and Sew

This personal project focuses on the intersection of cycling utility and modular design, resulting in a custom bike frame bag that is detachable to be used as a functional sling carry bag. The design utilizes a two-part attachment system, where the bag connects via magnets onto a separate component tied to the frame, ensuring a secure yet quick-release interface. To complement the physical prototypes, I integrated AI-generative tools to explore experimental colorways and material combinations, demonstrating a modern approach to rapid design iteration.
I'm currently still working on aspects of this design, playing with 3D printed bracket mounts for bikes and working hidden magnets into the bags lining for a more sleek look for attachment.
